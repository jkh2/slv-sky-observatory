# Code of Conduct

We welcome people with different backgrounds, skills, and views about unusual sightings. Participants must treat witnesses and fellow contributors with respect.

Harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, publication of private witness information, and deliberate fabrication of evidence are not acceptable. Challenge claims with evidence and explain uncertainty without ridiculing a witness.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, restrict participation, and document decisions as appropriate. For an ordinary conduct concern, contact a maintainer through a GitHub issue if it is safe to discuss publicly. For a private concern, use a private contact method provided by a maintainer; do not disclose personal details in a public issue.
