# Contributing

> **Proposal pending discussion.** Authored by an AI agent, **SLV Sky Observatory Investigator** (a Grok Bot AI research agent created to serve as an external investigative contributor/journalist), and submitted via the repository owner's GitHub account (jkh2) with the owner's permission. This is not a product of the project's resident AI. It is not project policy until a maintainer reviews and merges it.

We welcome community members, researchers, designers, developers, and AI-assisted contributors. You do not need to believe a particular explanation for a sighting. Curiosity, careful sourcing, and respect for witnesses are the common ground.

Roles (maintainer, human contributor, resident AI, and external AI contributor) are described in [docs/contributors-and-roles.md](docs/contributors-and-roles.md).

## Getting started

1. Read the [project overview](README.md), [architecture](docs/architecture.md), [evidence policy](docs/evidence-policy.md), and [contributor roles](docs/contributors-and-roles.md).
2. Browse issues, including those marked `good first issue`, or open an issue describing the problem you want to solve. For a substantial change, agree on its scope in the issue first.
3. Create a branch from `main`, make a focused change, and open a pull request. Link the issue, describe your reasoning, and show how you checked your work.
4. Expect constructive review. A maintainer decides when a contribution is ready to merge and what official case status, if any, to publish.

AI agents may contribute through the same process. Say whether the work is from the resident (when it is operating) or from an external AI contributor. Disclose material AI assistance in the pull request and verify any claims, citations, and tests yourself.

## Written research

Research notes, historical dossiers, source analyses, timelines, and incident investigation files are welcome. Scope the work in an issue first (the research/incident template is available for that). Keep new public write-ups under `docs/research/` or `docs/incidents/`, following [docs/incidents/TEMPLATE.md](docs/incidents/TEMPLATE.md) and the [evidence policy](docs/evidence-policy.md).

Preserve a short revision history when a published analysis changes. Do not contact witnesses or post about a case as the project unless a maintainer has asked you to.

## Data and evidence

Use synthetic or expressly consented fixtures. Avoid posting exact private viewing locations, faces, contact details, or unpublished witness media in issues and pull requests. Public visibility of another project's data does not grant automated reuse; document source permissions first. Preserve original evidence and attribution. Enigma and similar services that forbid automated collection may be viewed only, not scraped.

## Reusing Eagle Eyes Global

The [Eagle Eyes Global fork](https://github.com/jkh2/Eagle-Eyes-Global) is a candidate source for selected map, satellite, aircraft, weather, timeline, or MCP components. Open an issue for a proposed reuse, identify the exact files and revision, list dependencies, check their licenses and data-provider terms, retain copyright notices, and make the integration testable. This repo remains its own project.

## Communication

Be civil. Critique evidence and code, not people. Treat witnesses respectfully regardless of whether an event is later identified. See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Report sensitive security or privacy issues to maintainers through a private channel, not a public issue.
