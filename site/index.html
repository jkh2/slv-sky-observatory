01 / THE MISSION

Something was seen.
What can we learn?

People across the valley have stories, photographs, and questions. We’re building the place to bring them together with the best context we can find.

Some events may have ordinary explanations. Others may remain unresolved. Either way, the record should be honest about what was observed and what we still don’t know.

03 / HOW WE WORK

A record you can
return to.

One sighting may begin as a few seconds in the sky. A careful investigation can take much longer.

◌

Witness

Record the time, place, direction, account, and original media without turning a guess into a measurement.

⌁

Context

Compare with available aircraft, satellites, weather, astronomy, and earlier reports. Show where data is missing.

◇

Continuity

A persistent AI resident helps investigate and revisit cases, with sources and corrections kept in view.
