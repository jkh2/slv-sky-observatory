OPEN SOURCE / FOR BUILDERS

Build a better way
to look closer.

A shared project for people who care about careful observations, good tools, privacy, and questions worth investigating.

01 / THE SYSTEM

Many signals.
One traceable record.

The reporting app is the source of truth for cases. Public data adapters add context. A resident AI works from its own VPS Home through scoped tools. Portable pods can join when funded. Every derived claim should lead back to its evidence.

APP / 01

Witness experience

Secure accounts, fast reporting, observer location and direction, original media, and deliberate public visibility choices.

DATA / 02

Context engine

Satellite, flight, weather, astronomy, and historical sources with timestamps, rights, coverage, and uncertainty attached.

RESIDENT / 03

Continuing inquiry

A Codex CLI Home on its own VPS, model-agnostic cognition, durable memory, and authenticated MCP/API access to cases.

PODS / 04

Field instruments

Future portable, calibrated sensors at consenting sites, adding time-synchronized observations to existing cases.
