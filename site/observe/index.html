YOUR VIEW MATTERS / FOR OBSERVERS

A good report begins
with what you saw.

You do not need to know what it was. Your account, with its time and viewing direction, can help us ask the right questions.

01 / FIELD GUIDE

Notice the details.
Keep the original.

If you see something unusual, first make sure you are safe. Then capture what you can. An approximate answer is useful when it is clearly marked approximate.

TIME / 01

When did it happen?

Note the date, start time, duration, and whether the time is exact or estimated. Keep your phone's original file if you recorded it.

PLACE / 02

Where were you?

Mark where you stood, not where you think the object was. Your exact location can stay private when the reporting app opens.

DIRECTION / 03

Which part of the sky?

Remember a landmark or compass direction. Was it near the horizon or overhead? Trace its apparent path if you can.

RECORD / 04

What changed?

Describe color, shape, brightness, sound, and movement in your own words. Keep original photos and video, including footage before and after the event.

02 / WHEN REPORTING OPENS

Your account.
Your perspective.

The future app will let you sign in, place an observer pin, describe your sighting, and choose how much of your location and media to share. Investigators and the resident AI will compare reports with available context while preserving the original account and showing uncertainty.

  1. 01Submit your observation and original media through the app.
  2. 02Choose what is public and whether investigators may contact you.
  3. 03Follow the case, see evidence-linked findings, and add corrections.
