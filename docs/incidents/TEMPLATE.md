## How to read this file

- **Source observations** are what was reported as seen or recorded, attributed to a role, with uncertainty.
- **Pointers** (a reviewer flagging a time in a video or a comment to check) locate work to do. The reviewer's interpretation is not an analysis input.
- **Derived analysis** is computed or sourced context. A candidate is not an identification unless the evidence is sufficient.
- **Leads** are unverified unless a check is recorded.
